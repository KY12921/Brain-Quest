Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

version5 is a study app with lessons, games and an AI tutor. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app keeps, why, and who it's shared with.

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, the display name you choose, your grade band (Elementary, Middle or High School) and your language setting. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication — the app itself never sees or stores it.
  • Things you type or upload: messages in Global Chat and Battle Chat, feedback you send, messages you report, what you type to the AI Tutor, courses you generate in Study Mode, and photos you upload to Photo Help (Ultra).

Information the app creates as you use it

  • Learning progress: XP, level, completed lessons, streaks, missions, badges, league, and which subjects you find easier or harder.
  • Game items: coins, items you buy or are gifted, and what you have equipped.
  • Social features: your friends list, people you've blocked, your clan, and battle results.
  • If you pay for something, a Stripe customer reference so your subscription can be managed. Card details go directly to Stripe; the app never receives them.
  • Preferences such as theme, background and music are saved in your browser on your own device, not in your account.

What other players can see

  • Other signed-in players can see your display name, XP, league, number of completed lessons and equipped cosmetics (on leaderboards), your chat messages, and your name and score in battles and clans.
  • Other players cannot see your email address or the rest of your account.
  • Please don't put personal information — like your full name, school, age, address or phone number — in your display name or in chat.

Services that help run the app

The app uses these services, and the information they need is sent to them to make the app work:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and the database that stores your account and progress).
  • Val Town (hosts the app's server code that handles purchases, rewards, clans, friends and AI requests).
  • Google Gemini API (AI features: tutor chat, explanations, Kiwi's hints, Study Mode courses and Photo Help). What you type or upload for these features is sent to Google to generate the response.
  • Stripe (payments).
  • GitHub Pages (hosts the website) and Google Fonts (loads the fonts).

The app does not show third-party ads, and we don't sell your personal information.

Children

version5 offers an Elementary option, so some users may be children. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account. Parents or guardians can ask us to review or delete their child's account at any time (see "Contact" below).

Keeping and deleting information

Your account and progress are kept while your account exists. AI Tutor conversations are not saved to your account (they reset when you reload). To have your account and its data deleted, contact us as described below.

Changes

If the app starts collecting something new, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Use the Feedback page inside the app, or reach us on our Discord.
